Campus Emergency Procedures

Active Threat Situation

RUN - If you can get away safely

  1. Have an escape route and plan in mind
  2. Evacuate regardless of whether others agree to follow
  3. Leave your belongings behind
  4. Keep your hands visible as you exit
  5. Call 911 when you are safe

HIDE - If evacuation is not possible

  1. Lock and/or barricade the door
  2. Silence your phone and other electronic devices
  3. Hide behind large objects if possible
  4. Remain very quiet
  5. Do not respond to anyone at the door

FIGHT - As a last resort

  1. Only when your life is in imminent danger
  2. Attempt to incapacitate the threat
  3. Act with physical aggression
  4. Improvise weapons or use items available
  5. Commit to your actions

Severe Weather Emergency

Tornado Warning

  1. Seek shelter immediately in the lowest level of the building
  2. Go to an interior room or hallway without windows
  3. Get under a sturdy piece of furniture if possible
  4. Use your arms to protect your head and neck
  5. Remain in shelter until the all-clear is given

Flash Flood Warning

  1. Move to higher ground immediately
  2. Avoid walking or driving through flood waters
  3. Just 6 inches of moving water can knock you down
  4. 1 foot of moving water can sweep your vehicle away
  5. Stay away from bridges over fast-moving water

Fire Emergency

If you discover a fire

  1. Activate the nearest fire alarm pull station
  2. Evacuate the building immediately
  3. Close all doors behind you as you leave
  4. Use stairs, not elevators
  5. Call 911 once you are in a safe location

If trapped in a room

  1. Close the door and seal cracks with wet cloth if possible
  2. Call 911 to report your exact location
  3. Signal from window with light-colored cloth or flashlight
  4. Stay low where the air is less toxic
  5. Wait to be rescued by emergency personnel

Medical Emergency

First Response

  1. Check the scene for safety before approaching
  2. Call campus security at (123) 456-7890 or 911
  3. Provide your exact location and nature of emergency
  4. Do not move the victim unless absolutely necessary
  5. Remain with the victim until help arrives

CPR Guidelines

  1. Check responsiveness - tap and shout "Are you okay?"
  2. Call for help and activate emergency response
  3. Open airway with head-tilt chin-lift maneuver
  4. Check for breathing for no more than 10 seconds
  5. Begin chest compressions if no normal breathing

Hazardous Materials

Chemical Spill

  1. Alert people in the immediate area to evacuate
  2. If spilled material is flammable, turn off ignition sources
  3. Attend to injured or contaminated persons first
  4. Evacuate to a safe distance upwind from the spill
  5. Call campus security and provide details of the spill

If exposed to hazardous materials

  1. Flush exposed skin with water for at least 15 minutes
  2. Remove contaminated clothing carefully
  3. For eye exposure, flush eyes at eyewash station
  4. Seek medical attention immediately
  5. Report the exposure to your supervisor/instructor
